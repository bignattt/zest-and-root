Ingredient story

It starts underground

Ginger is the root of everything we make. Its warmth is sharp and a little sweet, so we pair it with citrus that is bright enough to match.

Our concept recipes keep the list short: a root, a fruit or two, and nothing else. This is the idea the whole brand is built around.

FAQ

Good questions

Concept answers for a fictional brand.

What does cold-pressed mean?

Cold-pressed juice is squeezed with pressure instead of being heated, so the flavor stays fresh.

How spicy is it?

Root Fire has the most kick, with ginger and a pinch of cayenne. Sunrise Zest and Green Citrus are milder.

How should I store it?

In this concept, bottles live in the fridge and are best finished within a few days of opening.

Can I buy it?

Not yet. Zest & Root is a fictional brand made for a design portfolio, so there is nothing to purchase.

Who designed this?

Victory, a graphic and product designer, built it as a portfolio concept. The design system is in design-system.html.
