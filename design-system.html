Spacing

Usage rules

  • Space inside components: 8 / 16 / 24.
  • Gaps between cards and grid items: 16 on mobile, 24–32 from 768px.
  • Section padding: 64 on mobile, 96 from 768px.
  • Page side gutter: 16 on mobile, 32 from 768px. Content max width 1152px.
  • Minimum tap target: 44px.

Corner radius

Four values only. Rounder shapes feel friendlier and echo the fruit.

Motion

Subtle and quick. Motion confirms an action or reveals content and never decorates for its own sake. It is turned off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

TokenValueUsed for
--durationFast150msButton color and press, link color, arrow nudge
--durationBase250msCard lift, icon tilt, FAQ open
--durationSlow600msScroll reveal, ingredient shapes
--easeOutcubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)All transitions

Rules

  • Hover lifts cards by 4px (--space05) and deepens the shadow.
  • Scroll reveal: fade in while moving up 24px, once only, with a 100ms stagger between siblings (data-reveal).
  • Hero bottles float 8px on a 6s loop. This is the only looping animation.
  • Under prefers-reduced-motion, content is visible immediately and nothing moves.
